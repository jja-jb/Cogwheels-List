BRIGHTSHELF PLATFORM SPECIFICATION 004
Identity

===========================================================================

004-001 SNAPSHOT

Identity is an Application Support link-state snapshot.

004-002 CONTACT IDENTITY

The contact identity is `CNContact.identifier`.

004-003 SHEET IDENTITY

The Sheet identity is `cw-` plus the 12-hex sheet ID selected by header name.

004-004 CW ID STABILITY

Cogwheel never rewrites `cw_id`.

004-005 CW ID VALIDATION

Cogwheel fail-stops on a missing or duplicate `cw_id`.

004-006 ID MODEL

The identity model is the `CNContact.identifier` plus `cw-` model in
`004-002` through `004-005`. Container scope and the exclusion of unified-store
synchronization are governed by `017-001` through `017-006`; this requirement
adds no unified-store identity rule.
