BRIGHTSHELF PLATFORM SPECIFICATION 009
Size Bound

===========================================================================

009-001 FORMULA

`max_bytes = 1 MiB + 1 KiB*(C+G+T) + 256 B*A`.

009-002 VARIABLES

`C` is live contact links, `G` is live group links, `T` is tombstones not yet GC-ed, and `A` is live field atoms plus live membership atoms.

009-003 PHOTO ATOM

The photo hash/presence is included in `A` as one ordinary ancestor.

009-004 INTEGRITY

Cogwheel SHA-256 hashes every ancestor.

009-005 OVERFLOW

Cogwheel fails closed on overflow.

009-006 GC LOAD

`N=3` is the load-bearing GC run count.
