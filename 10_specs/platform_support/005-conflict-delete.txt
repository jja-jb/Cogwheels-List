005-001 ANCESTOR

Conflict resolution is three-way against the last-successfully-applied ancestor.

005-002 ONE-SIDE CHANGE

One-side changes propagate.

005-003 EQUAL SIDES

When both sides are equal, the ancestor advances.

005-004 GENUINE CONFLICT

A genuine conflict freezes the field and records a header-named `cw_conflict`.

005-005 WINNER

There is no auto-winner.

005-006 MISSING LINK

Missing-after-live-link is a delete.

005-007 TOMBSTONE GC

Tombstone garbage collection occurs after 3 consecutive both-absent successful runs.
