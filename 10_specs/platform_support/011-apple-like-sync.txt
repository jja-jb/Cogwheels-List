011-001 PRODUCT SHAPE

Cogwheels builds the most Apple-like way to sync.

011-002 V1 SYNC

Version 1 behaves like iCloud Contacts and is bidirectional.

011-003 HUMAN UI

Contacts.app is the human UI; Cogwheel has no editing UI.

011-004 LOCKED SCHEMA

The locked schema is in place from day one and includes contacts, groups, membership, and photos as hash/presence.

011-005 PRODUCT BOUNDARY

Version 1 is not one-way export and is not contacts-only with groups later.

011-006 ENGINEERING SEQUENCING

Engineering may sequence internal slices.

011-007 SHIPPED PRODUCT

The product does not ship a one-way or contacts-only version 1.
