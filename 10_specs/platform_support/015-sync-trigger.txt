BRIGHTSHELF PLATFORM SPECIFICATION 015
Sync Trigger

===========================================================================

015-001 REQUIRED TRIGGERS

Cogwheel runs automatic synchronization when Apple Contacts change and when
the app launches.

015-002 NO REQUIRED TIMER

The product does not add a required timer-based sync trigger.

015-003 NO SHEET-SIDE POLL

The product does not add a required Sheet-side polling trigger.

015-004 NOT MANUAL-ONLY

The product is not manual-only. Apple Contacts changes and app launch are the
locked automatic entry points for a sync run.

015-005 ENGINEERING CONCERNS

Serialization, coalescing, and single-run-at-a-time behavior are engineering
under this trigger lock. They are not new product policy.

015-006 FLOW ENTRY

Each locked trigger enters the existing sync logic flow. The trigger does not
change identity, conflict, delete, group, membership, photo, header, size,
determinism, or editing-surface rules.
