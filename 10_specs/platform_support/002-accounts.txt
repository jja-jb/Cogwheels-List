BRIGHTSHELF PLATFORM SPECIFICATION 002
Accounts

===========================================================================

002-001 ACCOUNT SET

Version 1.x supports one Apple account plus one Google account.

002-002 GOOGLE SHARING

Google sharing is represented by external Sheet-side values.

002-003 MACHINE COLUMNS

Cogwheel is the only writer of machine columns.

002-004 INSTANCE SAFETY

Cogwheel fail-stops on a second instance fingerprint.
