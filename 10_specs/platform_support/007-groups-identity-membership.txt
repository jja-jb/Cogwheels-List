BRIGHTSHELF PLATFORM SPECIFICATION 007
Groups Identity and Membership

===========================================================================

007-001 GROUP IDENTITY

Groups are a second identity kind.

007-002 GROUP NAMESPACE

Group identity uses a separate `cw-` namespace.

007-003 MEMBERSHIP

Membership atoms are in scope.

007-004 SUBGROUPS

Subgroups are macOS-only.

007-005 CONTAINERS

Containers are write-target-only unless one container is selected as both the
read set and write target under `017-001` through `017-006`.
