BRIGHTSHELF PLATFORM SPECIFICATION 014
Conflict Unfreeze

===========================================================================

014-001 EXISTING FREEZE

After a genuine three-way conflict, Cogwheel records a header-named
`cw_conflict`, chooses no auto-winner, and provides no in-app winner button.

014-002 HUMAN RESOLUTION

The human resolves the named field by writing a new intended value in
Contacts.app, the Apple editing surface. The new Contacts value need not equal
the pre-freeze Sheet value.

014-003 CLEARING EVENT

A new human write to the named field in Contacts.app is the event that clears
the freeze. Merely observing the old Contacts value does not clear it.

014-004 SHEET-ONLY EDIT

Editing the Sheet cell alone does not clear `cw_conflict` and does not create a
winner. Cogwheel never auto-selects between the two frozen values without a
new Contacts write.

014-005 CONTACTS PROPAGATION

After the new Contacts write, Cogwheel propagates that live Contacts value to
the Sheet. The value is the intended human resolution for that named field.

014-006 SUCCESSFUL UNFREEZE

When propagation to the Sheet succeeds, Cogwheel clears `cw_conflict` and
advances the last-successfully-applied ancestor to the propagated Contacts
value.

014-007 SHEET WRITE FAILURE

If the Sheet cannot be written, the field remains frozen and `cw_conflict`
remains recorded until the Contacts value can be propagated successfully.

014-008 NO THIRD EDITOR

Cogwheel has no data-editing UI. The unfreeze action is a human write in
Contacts.app followed by the defined relay behavior.
